Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026 | Effective immediately

Welcome to dhshishir.com (including the Fluency Lab learning hub and personal portfolio of Delowar Hassan Shishir). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our services or sign in using Google OAuth / Supabase Authentication, we collect only the minimal data necessary to deliver our educational experience:

  • Account Identification: Your name, email address, and profile picture provided by Google OAuth.
  • Educational Records & Learning Data: Diagnostic quiz scores, CEFR fluency level assessments, recorded phoneme drill metrics, streak counts, and learning history.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, approximate device information, and standard web access logs used solely for security and performance optimization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Create, secure, and maintain your personal learner account.
  • Store and adapt your personalized spoken English diagnostic roadmap and weak sound radar across sessions.
  • Provide customer support and respond to direct inquiries sent via our contact forms.
  • Ensure platform integrity, prevent abuse, and deliver fast, secure page loads.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data. Your information is only shared with trusted infrastructure providers that power our platform under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Identity Services: For secure 1-click user authentication.
  • Supabase: For encrypted cloud database storage and authentication management.
  • Cloudflare: For secure content delivery and DDoS mitigation.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You may request full deletion of your account and all associated test records at any time by contacting us at contactwithshishir@gmail.com. Upon receipt, your personal records will be permanently purged from our database within 48 hours.

5. Security

All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Database access is protected with strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring learners can only access their own private data.
