Welcome to dhshishir.com (the "Website", "Portal", or "Platform"), owned and operated by Daloyar Hassan Shishir. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our visitors, learners, and academic researchers.

This Privacy Policy document details the types of information collected, stored, and used by dhshishir.com and outlines your rights regarding your personal information, cookies, and digital identity.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide educational simulators, diagnostic assessments, and platform features:

  • Personal Information (Voluntary): When you create an account via Google OAuth or email, or submit a message via our contact/advisory form, we collect your name, email address, profile picture (via Google Identity), and any message content you provide.
  • Educational & Telemetry Data: Diagnostic test scores (IELTS Listening/Reading/Writing OCR evaluations, Fluency Lab CEFR speaking assessments, Executive Course completions), daily study streaks, and unlocked achievement badges.
  • Log Data & Device Telemetry: Standard browser user-agent, IP address, approximate geographical region (country-level), referring pages, and access timestamps.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

Mandatory Advertising Disclosure: We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements when you visit our website.

In accordance with Google's Advertising Policies and requirements:

  • Third-Party Vendor Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visit to dhshishir.com and/or other sites across the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices/.
  • For more information on how Google uses data when you use partner sites, visit Google's Privacy & Terms Partner Site Policy.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other modern website, dhshishir.com uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, study session states, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites.

4. How We Use Collected Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our interactive learning engines and diagnostic tests.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our educational curriculum and intelligence dossiers.
  • Understand and analyze how visitors utilize our study tools and simulators.
  • Issue cryptographically verifiable certificates and badges to authenticated learners.
  • Prevent fraud, secure our servers, and ensure continuous platform availability.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights (European Users)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to data portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you.

6. CCPA Privacy Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business that collects personal data disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer that a business collected.
  • Request that a business that sells personal data not sell the consumer's personal data. (Note: dhshishir.com NEVER sells personal data to any third party).

7. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting children while using the internet is a priority. dhshishir.com does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.
